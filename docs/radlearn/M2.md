# M2 versioned approval

Finding edits append immutable snapshots. An approval pins finding ID, version,
canonical SHA-256, author and time. Both JSON (atomic rename with a per-case lock)
and Mongo (document revision compare-and-set) use the cases store seam. Stale
versions and hashes fail with 409. Compile is additive and keyed by take plus
marker/segment, and retrying a compile cannot overwrite expert edits.

Legacy findings migrate to v1 Needs review without approvals. Complete
Study/Series/SOP/frame identity must resolve against the archive. Index-only or
missing instances cannot be approved. Approval also requires a clean text and
metadata check and explicit pixel/audio/screen review; this does not claim OCR
or speech recognition. Published cases remain browsable, but their findings
lose tutor and exam grounding until explicitly reviewed and approved.

Tutor, private exam keys, teaching pages and live-pack reads use approved current
versions. Old case-level diagnosis and teaching prose are excluded from that
projection. Finding cards show approval state and incomplete anchors. Review
APIs are author-only and tenant-scoped; the production demo 403 is unchanged.

The hash already includes text, rubric, geometry, anchor, view state, evidence,
media-review flags and extraction/inventory provenance. The addendum extends
order-revalidation logging and exhaustive content-invalidation acceptance tests.
